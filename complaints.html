Your Rights as a Consumer

As a consumer in Portugal, you have the right to:

  • Submit a formal complaint via the Electronic Complaints Book.
  • Receive a written response within 15 working days.
  • Escalate unresolved complaints to the relevant regulatory authority (ASAE).
  • Use alternative dispute resolution (ADR) entities recognised in Portugal.

Alternative Dispute Resolution

In the event of an unresolved dispute, you may contact an Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) entity. A list of accredited ADR entities in Portugal is available at: www.consumidor.gov.pt

EU consumers may also use the European Online Dispute Resolution platform: ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr
