Dear Hilo Team,

I'm a Berlin-based Senior UX/UI Designer with over 10 years of experience designing mobile-first digital products. Health data is one of the most complex things to make legible and human, and that challenge is exactly what draws me to Hilo.

Throughout my career at AKQA, Wunderman Thompson, Meta Design, and DHL, I've worked on products where the core design problem was the same one Hilo faces every day: taking dense, continuous data and turning it into something a person can understand and act on. I've led UX from research through to handoff, run Design Sprints, built scalable Figma design systems, and collaborated directly with engineering and product in fast-moving teams. Working autonomously is simply how I've operated for years.

What Aktiia is doing sits at an intersection I find genuinely exciting: clinical-grade technology brought into everyday life, where the real design challenge is not the hardware but the trust. Getting someone to understand what their blood pressure data means, what it means for them specifically, and what to do about it requires careful information architecture, clear visual language, and a deep respect for the user's emotional relationship with their own health. That is the kind of UX problem I want to spend my time on.
