About me.

I'm Ana, a product designer and illustrator with 10+ years building digital products across Europe. I started in brand studios like Meta Design, AKQA, and Landor & Fitch, moved into product design for startups and scale-ups, and now work independently from Lisbon and Berlin.

What I care about is design that actually works, clear interfaces, considered interactions, and products people want to use again. I work best close to the problem, embedded in a team, where the process can do its job without the usual distance between designer and decision.
