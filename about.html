AboutBerlin · Lisboa · Remote

Copenicker Studio, founded by Ana, is a consulting studio based between Berlin and Lisbon, working with agencies, startups, and founders around the world. With over 10 years of experience, Ana connects the dots between brand, design, and strategy.

Most recently, she has been working with startups at the forefront of ed-tech, e-commerce, and no-code, leading global and remote teams through brand refreshes. Previously, she developed visual identities and contributed to award-winning projects across contexts where culture and commerce meet — from a book for a renowned theatre to a catalogue for an airline. Her experience spans education, e-commerce, exhibition, and editorial design, across both startups and agencies.

She builds visual systems that push brands forward while engaging people in ways that spark attention and curiosity. Her expertise lies in creating scalable brand systems that connect strategy to design, translating ideas into flexible visual layers that can be easily used across product, marketing, and content teams.

She specializes in UX design, illustration, and AI tools.
