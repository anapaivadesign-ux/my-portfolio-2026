1. Services

The Studio provides UI/UX design, illustration, brand strategy, and consulting services. The scope of each project is defined in a written proposal or statement of work agreed upon by both parties prior to commencement.

2. Proposals & Agreements

All proposals are valid for 30 days from the date of issue. A project begins upon written confirmation and receipt of the agreed deposit. Verbal agreements are not binding.

3. Payment

  • A deposit of 50% is required before work begins on any project.
  • The remaining balance is due upon project completion and before final files are delivered.
  • Ongoing retainer work is invoiced monthly, payable within 14 days of invoice date.
  • Late payments may incur interest at 3% per month after the due date.

4. Revisions

Each project includes a defined number of revision rounds as specified in the proposal. Additional revisions beyond the agreed scope will be billed at the Studio's current hourly rate.

5. Timelines

8. Confidentiality

The Studio treats all client information as confidential and will not share it with third parties without prior written consent, except where required by law.

9. Cancellation
